# Stellaris 4.4.4 "Pegasus" Mechanics Overlay

<!-- Advisor-relevant stable 4.4.4 facts. Applied only to 4.4.4 and newer saves. -->

### Arkships, Colonies & Embarking
<!-- applies-to: nomadic -->
* **Arkship Habitability:** Arkships have inherent 40% habitability, matching habitats. Arkship Living Space Modules add +5% habitability and +1 district slot, but cost and upkeep Exotic Gases.
* **Arkship Modifiers:** Planet class, governor, colony, and scripted colony modifiers apply to Arkship-carried colonies. Do not assume Arkships ignore normal colony-side bonuses or penalties.
* **Embarking Armies:** Becoming Nomadic embarks assault armies rather than destroying them. A settled empire preparing to embark does not need to disband those armies first.
* **Doomsday Embarkation:** Doomsday empires that successfully embark gain a +60% habitability modifier, and Arkship Preparation reduces decision timing rather than building timing.

### Logistics, Contracts & Reserves
<!-- applies-to: nomadic -->
* **Collection Feedback:** Idle Arkships and Logistic Ships notify the player after finishing Waystation or Wayline stockpile collection, and the galaxy map has a Logistics Hub indicator.
* **System Orders:** Arkship and Logistic harvest or strip-mine orders issued on a whole system target higher-value planets first rather than merely the nearest planet.
* **Waystation Stockpiles:** Tribute Vault and Pirate Hideout Waystation modules add their resources to the Waystation stockpile instead of silently adding them to the empire stockpile.
* **Contract Aging:** AI empires may abandon a contract after 20 years and do not accept unreachable contracts. Do not treat old or unreachable contracts as guaranteed long-term income.
* **Contract Abandonment:** Abandoning Forever Cruise contracts penalizes Passenger Satisfaction, so dropping contracts is not free for that origin.
* **Operational Reserves (4.4.4):** Energy and Mineral reserve tracking uses a 3:1 rule rather than one-to-one conversion. Inspect the reserve tooltip before translating ordinary resource values into reserve health.
* **Strategic Harvesting:** Researching the relevant strategic-resource mining technology grants Nomads +50% harvesting yield for Volatile Motes, Exotic Gases, Rare Crystals, and Living Metal, including resources inherent to planet class.

### Science, War & Origins
* **Science Automation:** Automated Science Ships that temporarily run out of reachable tasks stay automated and resume when new work appears instead of cancelling automation.
* **Stellar Ignition Contracts:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Stellar Ignition Contracts are completable. Do not warn that this contract type is impossible because of the early 4.4 bug.
* **Nomad Ownership Changes:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Total Wars between Nomads move Arkships between owners, and Nomad Raids transfer Waystations on status quo in both directions.
* **Wayline Stacking:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Arkships do not benefit twice from Waystation network or Wayline modifiers. Avoid double-counting Wayline effects when evaluating Arkship output.
* **Forever Cruise:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Difficulty pressure and starting deficits are reduced, Passenger upkeep and early Specialist Crew pressure are lower, and the center situation stage is longer.
* **Baol Life Seeding:** Baol Life Seeding can be enacted on artificial worlds and structures, granting a unique deposit that carries over part of the Gaia-world bonus.
* **Deep Sleep:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Deep Sleep cannot be combined with Synthetic Fertility, and AI empires use Deep Sleep mechanics more reliably.
* **Compatibility Limits:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Shroud-Forged is blocked from Embarking. Do not promise that every settled-empire transformation can become Nomadic.
* **Fleet Persistence:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Fleets following a moving Arkship continue repairing, upgrading, and following after upgrades complete.
