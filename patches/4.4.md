# Stellaris 4.4 "Pegasus" Game Mechanics

<!-- Base Pegasus mechanics through the stable 4.4.3 hotfix. Later material changes are layered through exact patch-version overlays. -->

### Nomadic Empires & Arkships
<!-- applies-to: nomadic -->
* **Nomadic Empire Mode:** Nomadic empires are a distinct empire shape, not just a normal settled empire with a different authority, civic, or origin. They should not be judged by normal territory, colony count, or starbase expectations.
* **Arkships:** Arkships replace homeworlds as mobile capitals and act as colonies, shipyards, and warships. They have Military, Scientific, and Civilian classes, three tiers, modules, systems, and late-game Spinal Mount options.
* **No Standard Expansion Loop:** Nomads do not claim and colonize like settled empires. Their core loop is Arkship movement, Logistic Ships, Waystations, Waylines, contracts, settling decisions, and possible later embarkation from settled life.
* **Arkship Leaders:** Arkships can have both a colony-governor context and a ship-leader context. Advice should consider both colony modifiers and fleet/ship roles when interpreting Arkship strength.
* **Settling and Embarking:** Nomadic empires can settle once they unlock the relevant Adaptability or Versatility path. Settled empires can embark into Nomad life and convert starbases into Waystations. A settled empire that embarks should not be evaluated as if it still keeps normal borders and starbases.

### Waystations, Waylines & Logistics
<!-- applies-to: nomadic -->
* **Waystations:** Waystations harvest systems without claiming them. Nomad resource planning should prioritize good harvest routes, access, and pickup cadence rather than chokepoint ownership alone.
* **Waylines:** Adjacent Waystations form Waylines that propagate network benefits. Dense, connected routes are strategically stronger than isolated harvest points.
* **Wayline Pacts:** Wayline Pacts let settled empires and Nomads share network benefits. Trust, opinion, and contract history matter when evaluating access to these pacts.
* **Logistic Ships:** Logistic Ships collect Waystation and Wayline stockpiles and deliver them to Arkships. They replace construction ships for many Nomad tasks and are central to keeping the economy moving.
* **Civilian Arkship Economy:** Civilian Arkships and related actions support harvesting, extraction, and Planetary Reprocessor play. A Nomad economy can be healthy even when it does not resemble a settled mining-world economy.

### Contracts, Trade & Diplomacy
* **Contracts:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Settled empires issue Military, Science, Resource, and Diplomacy contracts. Contracts cost Trade to issue, provide Nomads with income and tasks, and can improve Opinion and Trust.
* **Contract Capacity:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Nomads are limited by Arkship capacity for active contracts, so contract advice should consider both rewards and available Arkship attention.
* **Diplomatic Engine:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Contracts are not just side quests. They are a core Nomad income and diplomacy engine that can unlock stronger relationships and Wayline Pacts.
* **Nomad Borders:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Nomads can often pass through closed borders until diplomatic or military action expels them. Border advice should account for expulsion risk rather than assuming normal territorial blockage.
* **Corporate Costs:** <!-- applies-to: corporate --> Branch Offices use Trade to establish and no longer scale establishment cost by distance. Expel Corporation rewards Trade per destroyed branch office.

### Operational Reserves & Nomad Economy
* **Operational Reserves:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Nomad Energy and Minerals flow through Operational Reserves. Red ordinary Energy or Mineral income is not automatically a crisis if reserves, stockpiles, contracts, and collection routes are healthy.
* **Resource Judgment:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> For Nomads, judge the economy by reserve level, stockpile collection, contract throughput, Waystation coverage, and Arkship actions before diagnosing a deficit.
* **Rarer Resources:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Nomad construction is designed around rarer resources and Arkship logistics more than continuous settled-planet mineral extraction.
* **Trade Pressure:** Trade is important for contracts, corporate actions, and the broader 4.x economy. Trade shortages can limit diplomatic and economic options even when Energy looks acceptable.

### Exploration, Science & Spinal Mounts
<!-- applies-to: nomadic -->
* **Scientific Arkships:** Scientific Arkships can Survey and Deep Scan. Deep Scan can reveal hidden resources, anomalies, archaeology sites, and, with Subspace Imaging, hidden hyperlanes or wormholes.
* **Science Ships Still Matter:** Arkships cannot directly research anomalies, archaeology sites, or Astral Rifts. Nomads still need Science Ships for those tasks.
* **Stellar Engine:** The Stellar Engine Spinal Mount injects Dark Matter into stars or gas giants to create or improve Energy and Physics deposits. Settled empires can contract Nomads for stellar ignition.

### War, Crisis & Late Game
* **Joining and Leaving Wars:** Non-primary war participants can negotiate to join or leave ongoing wars. Leaving creates a truce and diplomatic penalty, and the opposing side may refuse if it dislikes the offer or is winning.
* **Hopeless War Pressure:** Empires with all colonies occupied gain escalating Hopeless War pressure, increasing War Exhaustion gain and monthly war Attrition until at least one colony is liberated.
* **Arkship Warfare:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Arkships are not bombarded or invaded like planets. Hull damage functions as devastation, normal enemies disable Arkships, crises can destroy them, and Nomad Raids can seize Arkships or Waystations.
* **Nomad War Score:** <!-- applies-to: nomadic --> Wars against Nomads depend more on defeating fleets and disabling Arkships than occupying starbases. Do not assess Nomad wars with normal starbase-occupation assumptions.
* **Defender of the Galaxy:** <!-- applies-to: defender-ambition --> Defender of the Galaxy is a five-level Ambition path involving cooperation, Custodianship, GDF and Paladin mechanics, Hero Ships, and a victory route based on defeating two end-game crises, eliminating crisis empires, then abdicating.
* **Stellar Cannon:** <!-- applies-to: stellar-cannon --> The Stellar Cannon is an offensive megastructure requiring a neutron star or pulsar, no ascension perk, a long charge time, and the user's full Energy stockpile. High-energy shots devastate colonies and can dry out planets.
* **Fallen Empire Strength:** Fallen and Awakened Empire strength can be scaled in galaxy setup and also scales with difficulty. Late-game threat advice should not assume one fixed Fallen Empire fleet baseline.

### Jobs, Pops & Automation
* **No Unemployment:** Jobless pops immediately fall into their fallback category such as Civilian or Maintenance Drone. Advice should not plan around stockpiling unemployed pops.
* **Job Assignment:** Job assignment weighs pop suitability and job efficiency. Workers and Simple Drones reshuffle more often than Specialists, Complex Drones, and Elites.
* **Underpopulated Colonies:** Underpopulated planets distribute eligible pops proportionally across capped jobs, with designations, traits, and efficiency skewing the result.
* **Automation Slider:** Automated jobs have a workforce-limit slider. Automation advice should account for explicit automation caps, not just the presence of automation buildings.
* **Nascent Stage Pops:** Nascent Stage pops integrate gradually on sufficiently mature colonies. Do not treat them as immediately available full workforce.
* **Designation Strength:** Planet designation bonuses are stronger than the 4.3 baseline, and AI colony construction is more designation-driven.

### Nomad Origins, Civics & Traits
<!-- applies-to: nomadic -->
* **Voidfarers:** Voidfarers is the default flexible Nomad origin and should receive standard Nomad advice around Arkship movement, Waylines, contracts, and reserves.
* **Forever Cruise:** Forever Cruise uses Crew and Passenger species, Passenger Satisfaction, internal contracts, and failure states of Planetfall or Mutiny. Stable 4.4 hotfixes slow the situation and add a 24-month pause after completing a crew or passenger contract.
* **Heirs of the Khan:** Heirs of the Khan faces escalating bounty hunters and uses unique military contracts to weaken them. It is incompatible with other Ambitions such as Defender of the Galaxy.
* **The Sacred Path:** The Sacred Path revolves around sacred-site projects and spiritual choices. Advice should be origin-aware rather than generic Nomad logistics alone.
* **Void Reavers:** Void Reavers siphon through Waystations and can use Plundering bombardment. In stable 4.4.3, the civic no longer additionally requires First Contact.
* **Nomad Civics:** Nomad civic advice may need to account for Caravan Masters, Void Reavers, Star Seekers, Flight Schools, and Deep Sleep.
* **Nomad Traits:** Nomad trait advice may need to account for Photoadaptive or Solar Celled, Recursive Learners or Structural Awareness, Reaver, Terraphobic, and Interconnected.
* **Compatibility Limits:** Some origins and subject types are incompatible with Nomadic gameplay because they rely on fixed planets, systems, or specialist subject structures. Do not promise all settled-empire build plans can become Nomadic.

### Enclaves, Events & Support Notes
* **Champion's Forge Live!:** Champion's Forge Live! is a nomadic enclave built around recurring fleet tournaments, weight classes, a Trophy relic, combat intelligence, venue services, and Nomadic Paragon introductions.
* **Situation Log:** The Situation Log UI is reworked in 4.4. Situation-heavy builds should expect different organization and presentation than older versions.
* **Major Update Safety:** The launcher can auto-disable mods after major updates and incompatible resume attempts can divert to the main menu. For support and diagnostics, stale mods are a likely cause of broken post-update saves.
