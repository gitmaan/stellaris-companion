### Fleets, Armor and Equipment Preferences
* **Armor Upkeep:** Armor and same-tier shields have equal Energy and Alloy upkeep.
* **Equipment Preferences:** Defense Platforms have separate policies; Nomads use Arkship Weapon and Waystation Defense Preferences, without Starbase or Defense Platform policies.
* **Starbase Policies:** Preferences work with ship auto-design disabled; explosive preference requires Swarmer Missiles.

### Operational Reserves and Arkship Economy
* **Conserve:** The Operational Reserves Conserve approach imposes -50% Specialist Job Efficiency.
* **Arkship Components:** Equipped components contribute upkeep.

### Virtual Automation and Population Growth
* **Virtual Automation:** Fallen Empire buildings provide no automation for Virtual empires.
* **Clone Vats:** Ineligible Pops do not dilute assembly output.
* **Necrophage Growth:** Chamber of Elevation and House of Apotheosis stop base growth and Necrophage Bonus Growth.

### Cybernetic Ascension
* **Pilot Projects:** Bonuses are Cyborg-only Job Efficiency.
* **Democratic Transference:** Its district upkeep increase applies to all resources.

### Psionic Covenants
* **Cradle of Souls:** Gestalts receive Unity from Psionic Pops.
* **Whisperers Telepaths:** Each rank grants one of each research.
* **Covenant Bonuses:** Telepath bonuses apply immediately.

### Diplomacy and Subjugation
* **Franchising:** Subjugation proposals bypass Opinion, Trust and Embassy requirements, without guaranteeing acceptance.
* **Backed Revolts:** Successful revolting colonies join their supporter.

### Nomadic Knights of the Toxic God
* **Detoxification:** Taming or slaying the Toxic God enables Arkships to detoxify Toxic Terraforming Candidates.
* **Settlement:** Reaching its system permits settlement preserving the Order's Keep and Knights.
