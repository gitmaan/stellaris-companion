# Pegasus 4.4.6 Knowledge Audit

Audited on 2026-08-01 for the stable `Pegasus v4.4.6 (fdde)` build. This is a
coverage record for maintainers, not model-facing prompt content.

## Source Precedence

1. Stable Paradox release announcements establish intended public mechanics.
2. Shipped 4.4.6 defines, scripts, and English localisation resolve current values
   and changes made after an earlier announcement.
3. Unmodded save structure verifies how mechanics are represented in campaign data.
4. Open-beta notes are excluded unless the behavior is present in the stable build.

## Inclusion Standard

The snapshot is a correction layer, not a transcription of every patch note. A fact
belongs in it when it is current, strategically material, likely to be answered from
obsolete model knowledge, and broadly useful for interpreting Advisor or Chronicle
evidence. DLC mechanics receive compact recognition and guardrails when their absence
would cause a model to misread a valid save.

UI rearrangements, AI tuning, modding APIs, isolated event fixes, minor numerical
balance changes, and exhaustive origin/civic/trait catalogs stay out unless they
change common advice or campaign interpretation.

## Verified Corrections

- Pop Group identity can include species, category or stratum, ethos, and a faction
  when one applies. Early saves may omit the faction field before factions exist.
- 4.4.6 sets `LOGISTIC_POP_GROWTH_FLOOR = 0.1` and treats populations below 10
  as 10 for the logistic curve. The previous "no minimum growth floor" wording was
  misleading and has been removed.
- Colony establishment remains 100 population at 3 base progress per month, or
  about 34 months from zero.
- Normal pop-limited Assault Armies remain capped at one per 100 pops of the
  recruiting species; explicitly unlimited army types remain exceptions.
- Utopia, Synthetic Dawn, and the Humanoids Species Pack are integrated into the
  base game as of 4.3.6, while other DLC-specific mechanics still require active DLC.
- Anchorage, naval-capacity, Arkship-habitability, reserve, Zro-deficit, Stellar
  Cannon, and automation values in the snapshot were checked against shipped 4.4.6
  data rather than copied blindly from preliminary notes.
- The Stellar Cannon action has no 10,000-Energy firing minimum. Every shot spends
  the current Energy stockpile and can damage ships; 10,000 enables colony devastation.
- Full occupation adds a monthly stacking 5% country War Exhaustion gain modifier.
  The separate 1% monthly side Attrition applies only when the fully occupied empire
  is that side's main participant.
- Empire Focus category weights select Focus cards, while progression rewards add
  permanent research options; Focus selection does not reweight ordinary technology cards.
- Operational Reserves use the lowest 5% of current capacity for the Critical stage.
  One unmodified Tier II Civilian Arkship has 27,500 capacity: 15,000 base, 7,500
  from tier, and 5,000 from its Civilian role.

## Completeness Result

The snapshot is not, and should not become, a complete rules encyclopedia. This audit
establishes practical completeness by covering every headline 4.x system that can
change common Advisor advice or make Chronicle interpret valid campaign evidence as
impossible, then verifying hard claims against the final installed build. Lower-value
details remain available from campaign evidence and the model's general knowledge.

## Provider Evaluation

On 2026-08-02, the human-reviewed OpenRouter matrix recorded 84 production-limit
responses: 21 Advisor and Chronicle cases across DeepSeek V4 Flash, Claude Sonnet 5,
Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite, and GPT-5.6 Luna. There were no provider errors or structured
output fallbacks. Seventy-five responses were accurate or acceptable, nine were
concerning because of an unsupported secondary claim or Chronicle embellishment, and
none were materially incorrect on the requested decision or event.

Twelve focused responses were rerun after clarifying Empire Focus, Waystation
collection, and Chronicle evidence boundaries. Every rerun met its central review
criteria without a provider or schema failure. Some models still add nonessential
historical scene-setting, so the remaining risk is model variance in supporting prose,
not a repeated stale mechanic or a wildly incorrect recommendation.

The remaining risk is topic depth: a rare event chain, narrow civic interaction, or
specific component matchup may still need dedicated knowledge if users repeatedly ask
about it. Adding every such detail now would increase prompt cost for every request and
make superseded numbers harder to find during the next release audit.

## Future Audit Checklist

1. Confirm the exact stable build and checksum; never fold an open beta into stable.
2. Diff all official notes since the previous snapshot by gameplay domain.
3. Recheck every hard number in shipped defines, scripts, or localisation.
4. Add only material current-state facts and remove superseded prose.
5. Pin high-risk stale premises in unit tests and the human-reviewed provider eval.
