# Cygnus 4.5.1 Release-Note Coverage Audit

Reviewed on 2026-09-25 against the stable [4.5 release announcement and 4.5.1
hotfix](https://steamcommunity.com/app/281990/allnews/) and [Dev Diary #433's
release notes](https://forum.paradoxplaza.com/forum/developer-diary/stellaris-dev-diary-433-4-5-cygnus-preliminary-release-notes.1941572/).

The 4.5 and 4.5.1 files contain only mechanics explicitly supported by those
release notes. They omit numerical rules and older mechanics that have not been
checked against shipped 4.5.1 game data. In particular, 4.5 changes the Pop Group
save representation; the 4.4.6 snapshot's description of groups divided by Ethics
and Factions is no longer valid. The resolver therefore supplies these notes without
the older snapshot and labels the coverage partial.

A second pass over the complete Steam-posted release notes added consequential
rules for Cybernetic Job Efficiency, Hedonist growth, Consecrated Worlds, Biomorphosis,
Branch Office Influence, Stellar Cannon prerequisites, Operational Reserves, Deep
Scanning, Nomad Shroud accords, Total Wars, Purge Vassals, and Gateway ownership.
Federation pact persistence and ongoing-war participation rules are also included.
The 4.5.1 pack covers its gameplay hotfixes. Crash, UI, audio, and most one-off event
fixes are omitted because they do not change general strategic advice or extraction.

No 4.5 save or shipped 4.5 game data was available for extractor validation. The
app's ability to ingest a 4.5 save, including faction membership and federation
agreement reporting, remains unverified. This audit does not establish full 4.5.1
support. The briefing now passes through all selected policies using the existing
`active_policies` structure, without assuming Cygnus-specific policy IDs. Once game
data or a save is available, validate extraction, expand this into a self-contained
current-state snapshot, and record a new QA baseline.
