# Stellaris 4.4.5 "Pegasus" Mechanics Overlay

<!-- Advisor-relevant stable 4.4.5 facts. Applied only to 4.4.5 and newer saves. -->

### Economy & Operational Reserves
<!-- supersedes-rule: Operational Reserves (4.4.4) -->
* **Operational Reserves Override (4.4.5+):** Operational Reserves track Energy and Minerals one-to-one.
* **Reserve Capacity:** Maximum reserves start at 15,000 and gain 5,000, 7,500, or 10,000 per owned Tier I, II, or III Arkship. Each Civilian Arkship adds another 2,500 capacity per tier, and Resource Silo variants can raise the cap further.
* **Critical Reserves:** The Critical reserve stage imposes severe Research, Unity, and Alloy penalties. It also reduces Lithoid Happiness, growth, and Job Efficiency and reduces Mechanical or Machine Job Efficiency. Treat this stage as urgent only when the provided game state confirms it; otherwise describe the threshold conditionally and suggest checking the reserve tooltip.
* **Resource Abundance:** Galaxy setup has a Resource Abundance slider from Scarce to x5, with x2 as the default. Do not judge a galaxy's deposit density against a universal baseline when this setting is absent from provided game state.
* **Megastructure Collection:** Nomadic resource-producing megastructures require a Waystation to collect their output and do not also pay resources directly. Avoid double-counting their production.

### Automation, Logistics & Contracts
* **Per-Action Automation:** Fleet automation has per-action controls. Arkships and Logistic Ships can separately configure Harvest, Strip Mine, Deep Scan, target-ownership rules, depositless targets, and rescanning, and can save or apply defaults by ship type.
* **Arkship Exploration:** Arkships can use Explore automation to navigate toward Points of Interest.
* **Logistic Defaults:** Logistic Ship automation defaults to Harvest Resources on and Waystation collection off. A stalled stockpile pickup may require enabling collection rather than building another Logistic Ship.
* **Waystation Fauna Attacks:** Space fauna disable Waystations rather than destroying them. The Waystation re-enables after the fauna leaves or is destroyed.
* **Harvest Contracts:** A Harvest Resources contract is cancelled if another empire harvests the target first. Do not treat a contested contract as guaranteed income.
* **Nomad Megastructures:** Nomads can build megastructures in a system containing one of their Waystations even when that system has not been surveyed.

### Arkship Strategy
* **Arkship Migration:** Pops can migrate from Arkships. Do not treat an Arkship population as permanently immobile.
* **Aura Cooldown:** An Arkship has a 10-year cooldown after benefiting from a maximum-strength Aura effect, preventing rapid Aura chaining for early ascension levels.
* **Destructive Diplomacy:** Opinion penalties for strip-mining, planetary reprocessing, and Exodus Jumps are lower and decay faster, but these actions still carry diplomatic costs.
* **Arkship Weapon Districts:** Laser Capacitors, Flight Decks, Ammunition Lines, and Missile Silos add strategic-resource upkeep to Soldiers. Include that upkeep when recommending weapon-focused Arkship specialization.
