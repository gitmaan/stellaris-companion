# Stellaris 4.5.1 "Cygnus" Release-Note Mechanics

* **Nomad Synaptic Lathes:** A Synaptic Lathe that Nomads build in another empire's system remains owned by the Nomads rather than transferring to the Starbase owner.
* **Fleet Automation:** Automated fleets follow the actions and options selected by the player. The Harvesting Agreements Automation sub-menu and its Trade Action are absent.
* **Combat Disengagement:** Ships do not automatically disengage merely because they have not participated in combat for 15 days.
